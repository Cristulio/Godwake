## Compound interaction analysis (data-driven)

Blessing aggregator (#86) caps acBonus/critRangeBonus/damageBonus/holyDamageBonus/extraTempHpPerRoom at max-of-individual — that lid held. The interaction patterns the data actually surfaces:

- **No build reaches the Ch6 final boss (the-unmade)**: every scenario kills the Ch6 boss 0.0% of the time — the L7 AI-floor bot dies in Ch2–Ch5 before it gets there. So this matrix measures MID-RUN blessing stacking on the now-6-chapter run, not the endgame. The 6-chapter extension out-runs the floor (see the full-resim findings); read these as a stacking-interaction probe, not a completion test.
- **Optimal vs Pauper Wizard delta is mostly Ch1→Ch2, not Ch3+**: Optimal kills the Magistrate 31.8% vs Pauper's 4.6% (Δ 27.2pp), but both stall at the Ch3 Director (Optimal 1.2% vs Pauper 0.0%). The defensive stack is a Ch2-gate clear, not a late-game multiplier — Phase 1 buffs do NOT compound through the Ch3 Director wall.
- **Fighter / Rogue cross the Ch4 Matron (a mid-game boss now)**: Fighter kills the Matron 13.8% and Rogue 18.2%, at 100.0%/100.0% death. Both then die in Ch3–Ch5; neither reaches Ch6.
- **Rogue stalls at the Director's Hold Person + glaive-reach combo**: 18.6% of all rogue runs die in Ch3 with the full defensive stack on. DEX-save proficiency doesn't help against the Director's WIS DC 15 stilling. Stillness of the Mind's +1 WIS save (post-stacking-cap) is not enough.
- **Mage Armor + Shield reaction layering DOES NOT trivialise Wizard survival**: post-#86 caps, the wizard's 94.2% mid-game death rate shows Mage Armor (+3) + Steel (+1) + Shield reaction (+5 burst) buys two extra rooms, not a chapter. Validated by the Optimal-vs-Pauper ch-cleared gap of 0.41 chapters.

## Sim caveats

- **Boss intel choice is a no-op for combat outcomes today.** The engine records `bossIntel: 'partial' | 'full'` and `boldApproachBosses` flags but does not yet feed them into combat (informational HUD only) or into `goldDrop.ts` (the +5% bold-approach gold isn't wired). So Optimal's "scout-every-boss" and Fighter's "walk-past-every-boss" choices wash out in the sim — the only mechanical difference would have been Optimal losing 170g across the run, which doesn't translate to combat power because the sim doesn't visit shops. If the bold-approach gold bonus gets wired in Phase 2, re-run with intel choices modelled.
- **Shrines are skipped** to keep blessings deterministic per scenario. Real runs would let Optimal stack two MORE blessings (one per Ch1+Ch3 shrine pair = 4 picks across the delve). That's an under-estimate of Optimal's true ceiling — the data here is a LOWER bound on stacking power.
- **Shop spend is not modelled.** No potions purchased mid-run, no scout fees, no merchant items. Fighter's gold haul (~440 avg from kills) would normally buy a +2 attack item or potion stack; that's an extra power layer the sim doesn't capture.
- **Subclass selection auto-picks the single available option per class** (Evocation for wizard, Champion for fighter, Thief for rogue at L3). That matches today's content; if Phase 2 adds a second subclass per class, re-run with the new picks.
- **No gear DROPS / affixes are modelled.** Scenarios use fixed preset gear; the Wave 1/2 Diablo loot system (rolled affixes, rarity, drops, legendary banking) is not exercised. Real runs trade UP into rolled gear, so every survivability/throughput number here is a floor.
- **6-chapter floor.** The run is now 6 chapters (~65 rooms on a route); the bare L7 AI-floor bot dies in Ch2–Ch5, so this matrix can no longer probe Ch5/Ch6 boss tuning — it measures mid-run stacking only.

## Recommendation

**No tune this round.** All four scenarios sit within the class-balance-philosophy band:

- Optimal Wizard dies 100.0% — far above the 30% target, far above the 15% over-power flag, and never reaches the Ch6 final boss. Defensive stacking does NOT trivialise the run.
- Pauper Wizard clears Ch1 83.2% — far above the 5% accessibility floor. Boss tunings did NOT close off minimum-investment runs.
- Fighter Bruiser is the strongest of the four: 100.0% death, kills the Ch4 Matron 13.8%, but every step is bought with 197 dmg taken on average and it dies before Ch6 (final-boss kill 0.0%).
- Rogue Lurker dies 100.0% — within philosophy, with the Director (Ch3) doing most of the killing (18.6% of all runs die there).

Blessing/camp-boon stacks combine the way their authors expected (the #86 aggregator cap holds). The biggest interaction-risk surfaced is NOT over-power but the Wizard's Ch2→Ch3 cliff: even fully stacked, the Director's Hold Person ends 30.6% of Optimal Wizard runs in Ch3. Worth flagging for the balance backlog ("Director Hold Person tuning vs caster-archetype WIS saves") but NOT a feature-stacking issue. NB: with the 6-chapter extension, this matrix no longer reaches the endgame — Ch5/Ch6 tuning needs a competent-play or higher-start-level probe.

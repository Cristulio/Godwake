## Compound interaction analysis (data-driven)

Blessing aggregator (#86) caps acBonus/critRangeBonus/damageBonus/holyDamageBonus/extraTempHpPerRoom at max-of-individual — that lid held. The interaction patterns the data actually surfaces:

- **Optimal vs Pauper Wizard delta is mostly Ch1→Ch2, not Ch3+**: Optimal kills the Magistrate 53.2% vs Pauper's 15.0% (Δ 38.2pp), but both stall at the Director (Optimal 1.6% vs Pauper 0.0%). The defensive stack is a Ch2-gate clear, not a late-game multiplier — Phase 1 buffs do NOT compound through the Director→Matron wall.
- **Fighter is the lone class that crosses the Matron threshold**: 32.6% Matron kill at 67.4% death rate. Within philosophy (death > 30%) but ablation below shows which lever owns the run.
- **Rogue stalls at the Director's Hold Person + glaive-reach combo**: 62.8% of all rogue runs die in Ch3 with the full defensive stack on. DEX-save proficiency doesn't help against the Director's WIS DC 15 stilling. Stillness of the Mind's +1 WIS save (post-stacking-cap) is not enough.
- **Mage Armor + Shield reaction layering DOES NOT trivialise Wizard survival**: post-#86 caps, the wizard's 95.8% mid-game death rate shows Mage Armor (+3) + Steel (+1) + Shield reaction (+5 burst) buys two extra rooms, not a chapter. Validated by the Optimal-vs-Pauper ch-cleared gap of 0.51 chapters.
- **First-attack blessing stack on Fighter is the highest-leverage Phase 1 interaction**: Tempus Fury + Mystra Veil + Selûne Veil all target different fields and SUM (per aggregator). With Action Surge on round 1, Fighter L7 lands first-attack-flavoured bursts that scale across the whole run — likely the dominant contributor to the Matron-kill rate above. (Confirm via ablation row below.)

## Sim caveats

- **Boss intel choice is a no-op for combat outcomes today.** The engine records `bossIntel: 'partial' | 'full'` and `boldApproachBosses` flags but does not yet feed them into combat (informational HUD only) or into `goldDrop.ts` (the +5% bold-approach gold isn't wired). So Optimal's "scout-every-boss" and Fighter's "walk-past-every-boss" choices wash out in the sim — the only mechanical difference would have been Optimal losing 170g across the run, which doesn't translate to combat power because the sim doesn't visit shops. If the bold-approach gold bonus gets wired in Phase 2, re-run with intel choices modelled.
- **Shrines are skipped** to keep blessings deterministic per scenario. Real runs would let Optimal stack two MORE blessings (one per Ch1+Ch3 shrine pair = 4 picks across the delve). That's an under-estimate of Optimal's true ceiling — the data here is a LOWER bound on stacking power.
- **Shop spend is not modelled.** No potions purchased mid-run, no scout fees, no merchant items. Fighter's gold haul (~440 avg from kills) would normally buy a +2 attack item or potion stack; that's an extra power layer the sim doesn't capture.
- **Subclass selection auto-picks the single available option per class** (Evocation for wizard, Champion for fighter, Thief for rogue at L3). That matches today's content; if Phase 2 adds a second subclass per class, re-run with the new picks.

## Recommendation

**No tune this round.** All four scenarios sit within the class-balance-philosophy band:

- Optimal Wizard dies 100.0% — far above the 30% target, far above the 15% over-power flag. Phase 1 defensive stacking does NOT trivialise late-game.
- Pauper Wizard clears Ch1 86.4% — far above the 5% accessibility floor. Phase 1 boss tunings did NOT close off minimum-investment runs.
- Fighter Bruiser sits at 67.4% death / 32.6% Matron-kill — the strongest of the four, but every clear is bought with 192 dmg taken on average and Matron still kills 54.2% of attempts that reach her.
- Rogue Lurker dies 99.6% — within philosophy, with the Director (Ch3) doing most of the killing (62.8% of all runs die there).

Phase 1 features stack additively in the way their authors expected. The biggest interaction-risk surfaced was NOT over-power but the Wizard's Ch2→Ch3 cliff: even fully stacked, the Director's Hold Person ends 51.6% of Optimal Wizard runs. Worth flagging for the Phase 2 backlog ("Director Hold Person tuning vs caster-archetype WIS saves") but NOT a Phase 1 tune target — that's a chapter-tuning issue, not a feature-stacking issue.

# MASSIVE sim pass — full integrated-game balance picture (2026-06-01)

The big comprehensive validation of the whole game as it now stands: 14 chapters,
6 classes (incl. Druid), the full ascension + dungeon-twist system, and the
progression-pass leveling/curve. **Measurement only** — nothing was tuned. The
sim knobs (`ARCHETYPE`, `ASCENSION`) and `scripts/sim-xp-curve.ts` already
existed; no engine, content, or balance was changed and all generated
`docs/sim-findings/*` were restored after capture.

## Read this first — the AI-floor scope caveat (what these numbers CAN and CANNOT say)

The shared Auto-Battle bot (`chooseCombatAction`/`runAutoTurn`) **underplays a
real player**. Even with the full loot/camp/legendary economy modelled, only the
Fighter ever clears the 14-chapter chain, and most lives die in **Ch1-Ch2**
(reaching ~Ch3-4 at the deep end). The user has cleared the whole game by hand.

So this pass **validates**:

- **Class balance** — relative ordering on identical content (the robust signal).
- **The early-mid game** (Ch1-Ch4), where the bot actually lives and dies.
- **The meta-loop at scale** — reincarnation chain, Grove buys, ascension ladder,
  proc wiring (do the signature mechanics fire?).
- **Difficulty scaling** — does the ascension ladder rise monotonically, and do
  the gated threats (ascendant elites, boss second-wind, twists) bite?

It **cannot** say anything about **endgame clearability (Ch5-14 vs a real
player)** — absolute clear-rates and life-counts are an AI-floor artifact, not
game truth. A low clear-rate is NOT "broken." Endgame difficulty is a
**playtest-only** question. Read the ranking and the shape, never the magnitudes.

**Where each class hits the wall (death-by-chapter, balanced):** deaths cluster
overwhelmingly in **Ch1-Ch2** for every class, then fall off a cliff (souls that
survive Ch2 mostly keep going until much deeper). The top kill-room across nearly
every class is the same Ch2 pairing — `slaver-cuirassier + cult-fanatic` (and the
cult-fanatic packs generally). The wall is the **bare-soul early game** (each life
restarts at L1), not a mid-game content gap.

- **Fighter** — Ch1 352 / Ch2 508, then near-zero. The only class to push to the
  Ch5-6 boss with any regularity. Steady tanky climber; tops the ascension ladder.
- **Rogue** — Ch1-heaviest of the pack (Ch1 564 > Ch2 384): the L1 bare-soul wall
  is its signature, consistent with the Nimble Dodge floor buff softening but not
  erasing it. Mid-low pack, **not dead last** (wizard is lower) — the buff landed.
- **Wizard** — shallowest. Ch2 668 (front-loaded into the cult-fanatic wall). The
  known AI-floor caster handicap (the bot under-plays slots).
- **Barbarian** — deep non-clearer (~18 rooms). Notably the **fewest Ch1 deaths**
  (204-239) — the d12 hull + Rage soak carries the opening — but it has the most
  Ch5-6 deaths of any class (Ch6 51-64), i.e. it dies *later and deeper*.
- **Ranger** — deepest non-clearer (~20 rooms, and the only class aggressive
  *deepens*, to 21.9). Its weakness is purely the L1 floor; past it, it reaches
  among the deepest.
- **Druid** — sits with the Rogue in the mid-low band, **just above the Wizard**
  caster floor (depth 13.7 balanced vs 11.9). Holds the caster hard-tier as the
  5th class; introduces no distortion to the others (per-class seed isolation).

### Class balance read

The asymmetric band ([[dd-roguelite-class-balance-philosophy]]) holds. Floor
ordering by depth (balanced): **fighter 29.6 ≫ ranger 20.0 > barbarian 17.7 >
rogue 13.8 ≈ druid 13.7 > wizard 11.9.** No class is out of the intended
asymmetric band:

- **Fighter** is the AI-floor benchmark (tanky, the only clearer) — expected, not
  a runaway; its clear% is still single digits.
- **Wizard/Druid** anchor the caster floor — expected AI-floor caster handicap,
  the kit fires (3.6 casts/combat each), they are weakest only because the bot
  under-plays spell economy, not because the kit is weak.
- **Ranger/Barbarian** are the deep martial non-clearers; both gated by the L1
  bare-soul wall, not their kits.
- **Druid** lands exactly where the prior validation lane put it (just above
  Wizard) and shifts no other class — clean addition.
- **Rogue** is mid-low and clear of dead-last, confirming the Nimble Dodge buff
  did its job at the floor.

### Archetype effect

The cautious/balanced/aggressive spread is **narrow** and class-dependent:
cautious deepens the Fighter (32.0 vs 26.1 aggressive); aggressive deepens the
Ranger (21.9). Balanced is the house line and no archetype flips the class
ordering. No single-policy conclusion overrides the relative read.

## 3. Ascension scaling + twists at scale (`sim-feel`)

Difficulty rises **monotonically** A0→A6 across all three archetypes. Balanced
shown (cautious/aggressive track within ~1pt):

| Metric (balanced) | Asc 0 | Asc 3 | Asc 6 |
|-------------------|------:|------:|------:|
| Mean rooms reached | 44.8 | 33.6 | 30.8 |
| Overall blowout % (won, HP never <80%) | 68.1% | 65.0% | 58.8% |
| Mean min-HP (all fights) | 77.4% | 75.1% | 71.7% |

Reach falls, tension rises, every step. The gated threats bite at their gates:

| Fight kind | metric | Asc 0 | Asc 3 | Asc 6 |
|-----------|--------|------:|------:|------:|
| **elite** | win rate | 91.3% | 84.4% | 86.1% |
| | avg rounds | 3.98 | **5.46** | **5.87** |
| **boss** | win rate | 94.4% | 91.3% | 88.4% |
| | blowout % | 56.8% | 48.4% | **35.3%** |
| | mean min-HP | 69.8% | 62.3% | **55.2%** |
| | avg rounds | 3.13 | 3.52 | **4.20** |

- **Ascendant elites** (@≥2): elite fights stretch from ~4 to ~6 rounds while
  normals stay flat (~3.2) — the elite ascendancy is the bite, not a blanket ramp.
- **Boss second-wind** (@≥3): boss blowout collapses 57%→35% and boss min-HP
  drops 70%→55% — bosses get genuinely tense at high ascension.

### Twists (Asc ≥ 4, measured at Asc 6, normal rooms only)

Clean vs twisted normals (balanced): twisted blowout 68.3%→51.3%, min-HP
78.5%→69.8% — twists add real teeth without spiking win rate (96.4% vs 97.1%).
Per-twist (balanced, corroborated across all three archetypes):

| Twist | Win rate | Blowout % | Mean min-HP |
|-------|---------:|----------:|------------:|
| bloodscent | 96.9% | 59.2% | 72.5% |
| cursed-ground | **92.4%** | 47.2% | 67.0% |
| gloom | 97.6% | 54.2% | 72.5% |
| quickening | 97.6% | **40.0%** | 63.7% |
| sealed-wards | 97.4% | 56.4% | 73.5% |

- **Quickening** is the biggest tension lever (lowest blowout, ~40%) while keeping
  win rate high — healthy.
- **Cursed-ground** is again the lone **win-rate** dropper (~89-92% across
  archetypes vs ~96-98% for the rest) — consistent with the prior full-system
  finding that its flat 4/turn HP-agnostic chip is the one twist that converts to
  deaths rather than just tension.
- Gloom mildest. All twists are gated correctly (zero twisted rooms at Asc 0/3).

## 4. Tuning directions (no numbers, change nothing — for the user)

These are flagged directions only; this lane tuned nothing.

1. **Cursed-Ground twist** remains the one outlier across the whole matrix — the
   only twist that drops *win rate* (not just tension) at every archetype. Its
   flat HP-agnostic chip is worth softening (e.g. scaling to a fraction of max-HP
   or capping). Already sim-flagged twice; this run is the third corroboration.
2. **No other class- or system-level tuning is indicated.** The asymmetric band
   holds, Druid and the Rogue buff sit where intended, ascension scales cleanly,
   leveling pace is on target, and every kit fires.
3. **Endgame clearability (Ch5-14) is unmeasured here** and is the next real
   question — it is a **playtest** task, not a sim one, because the AI floor dies
   in Ch1-2.

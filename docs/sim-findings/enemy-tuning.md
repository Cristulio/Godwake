## 4. Verdict

**One clear spike, the rest in-band — but Ch3/Ch4 went untested.**

- **`famished-ghast` (Ch1, multiattack 2× + battle-rage) is over-tuned for a regular
  warmup-band enemy.** It is the single deadliest thing in Chapter 1 — 23.8% kill-share and
  34.9% lethality, essentially the chapter BOSS's lethality (`duergar-ilyich` 38.8%), and the
  top single-turn burst in the run (17, vs the global p99 of 15). A non-boss that kills as often
  as the boss is the prime candidate for the balance lane: trim a multiattack die, its battle-
  rage trigger, or its frequency in the early pool. (AI-floor caveat applies — a real player
  blocks/bursts it down better — so this is "look at it," not "emergency nerf".)
- **Every other new mechanic sits in a reasonable band.** Summon caps held (`duergar-taskmaster`
  peaked at its `maxActive` 2, 61 spawned / 48 killed — bounded, no runaway; adds landed only 7
  kills). Debuff attrition is modest (5.8% of deaths occurred while debuffed; `plaguebound-cur`
  poison and `cell-wight` weakened apply often but convert to few kills — 1.2% / 12.9% lethality).
  Sustain did not stall (`cowled-wardpriest` 6.0 rounds vs 5.4 same-chapter baseline; 0 timeouts).
  Non-ghast multiattack burst is unremarkable.
- **Coverage gap (important):** at Ascension 0 the auto-battle bot rarely clears Chapter 2, so the
  Ch3 (`asylum-fleshwright`, `mind-leech`, `sphere-aberration`, `gibbering-husk`) and Ch4
  (`spider-broodmother`, `drow-war-priestess`, `cavern-hunting-spider`) new enemies logged **zero
  appearances** — their mechanics (deep summon snowball, life-drain sustain, blind) are **unvalidated
  here**. A follow-up should seed souls directly into Ch3/Ch4 (or run at higher ascension with a
  Grove-loaded soul) to exercise them before trusting their tuning.

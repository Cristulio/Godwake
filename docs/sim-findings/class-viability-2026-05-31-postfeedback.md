**Measure-only.** No game mechanic, balance value, spell, class, monster, or gear
number changed in this lane. One run of `scripts/sim-class-viability.ts` at
`SOULS_PER_CLASS=150 MAX_LIVES=150` (its file-header standard sample, 154s wall).
The auto-generated tables live in `class-viability.md`; this file is the analysis
+ baseline diff and is NOT regenerated by the sim.

## What this sim can and cannot answer

`sim-class-viability` is the **loot-blind survival floor**: preset gear only, no
rolled affixes / drops / legendaries, shared Auto-Battle bot. It reports relative
depth / level / ascension reach and proc rates. It is the right instrument for
"do the new class mechanics fire?" and "relative floor ordering". It is **NOT**
the instrument for the two headline validation targets:

- **(a) Wizard endgame curve after the spell reworks** — needs `sim-endgame-gear`
  (gear/economy loop, souls reach L8+ where Lightning Bolt / scaling Magic
  Missile-Scorching-Ray / Vampiric Touch / Exsanguinate actually matter). At the
  loot-blind floor wizards die ~L5 (see below), so most reworked spells barely
  come online. The wizard-curve verdict from the last lane lives in
  `wizard-scaling-boss-resist.md` (geared: mean ascension 0.97 → 5.15, tops A6
  37.5%, still weakest of five).
- **(b) Control land-rate vs bosses under advantage-on-save** — this sim has **no
  control-save instrumentation at all**. Legendary-Resistance-as-advantage cannot
  be observed here. Needs the encounterStress matrix or a dedicated boss-control
  probe.

Both gaps are flagged, not closed — this pass ran only the one named sim.

### Reads

- **Wizard floor ticked up** (depth 20.5 → 24.9, level 4.31 → 5.02), moving it
  from mid-pack to 3rd, above both fighter and rogue. Plausibly the scaling
  Magic Missile / Scorching Ray and the focused Lightning Bolt give the L4-5
  wizard a bit more reach before it dies. **But this is the floor, not the
  curve** — the reworks are designed for L8+ play that this sim never reaches.
  Do not read this as the wizard-rework verdict; that requires the geared sim.
- **Rogue dropped sharply** (depth 20.3 → 13.7, level 4.16 → 3.21) and is now
  clearly the shallowest class, dying mostly in **ch1 (12,101 deaths, the largest
  single-chapter death pile of any class)**. Rogue's kit was untouched by this
  pass, so the most likely driver is the larger/longer sample shaking out the
  bare-soul L1 fragility rather than a regression — **but the magnitude is large
  enough to flag for a confirming run.** ⚠️
- **Barbarian / Ranger** unchanged in shape — still the two deepest at the floor.
  Barbarian's Rage (physical-damage halving every combat) keeps it the prime
  "watch the high side" candidate; the brief's Legendary-Resistance and spell
  changes don't touch its kit, and the numbers confirm no movement.

- **barbarian / ranger** both wall hard on `hollow-dawn` (ch5 boss: 3,764 / 3,698,

  reaches the final chapter and dies there.

## Verdict

Relative floor ordering holds: **Ranger ≈ Barbarian deepest, Wizard mid, Fighter
mid, Rogue now shallowest.** Signature mechanics all fire. The wizard floor
nudged up post-spell-rework but the **wizard endgame curve and boss control
land-rate — the two changes this brief most wanted validated — are not observable
in a loot-blind, ~L5-death sim** and need `sim-endgame-gear` + a control-save
probe respectively. Two things to flag (not fix): the **rogue depth collapse**
(20.3 → 13.7, large enough to want a confirming run) and **Barbarian still
top-of-floor** (the standing high-side watch). No numbers tuned.

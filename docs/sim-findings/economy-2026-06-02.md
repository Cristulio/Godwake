## 2. Reworked renown economy — PROGRESS-WEIGHTED as intended

Formula (confirmed in `delveStore.ts`, pre-multiplier base):

```
base = (clear ? 50 : 3)            # floor: clear=50, fail=3
     + 2  × mobsKilled             # RENOWN_PER_MOB_KILLED
     + 25 × bossesBroken           # RENOWN_PER_CHAPTER_BOSS
     + 1  × roomsReached           # RENOWN_PER_ROOM_REACHED
total = floor(base × soulMarkMult × ascensionRenownMult)
```

Representative payouts (A0, no soul-marks, mult = 1.0):

| Scenario | base renown |
|----------|------------:|
| Shallow death: 2 mobs, 3 rooms, no boss | **10** |
| Mid death: ~10 mobs, ~8 rooms, 1 boss | **58** |
| Deep death Ch5: ~30 mobs, 25 rooms, 4 bosses | **188** |
| Full clear Ch6: 50 floor + ~40 mobs + 30 rooms + 5 bosses | **285** |

Verdict: **the curve is now progress-weighted, not flat.** A shallow 2-mob death
pays single digits (10); progress (mobs, bosses, depth) is the dominant term and
a deep run / clear pays 20-30×. The clear bonus (50) is small relative to the
progress sum on a deep run — payout is earned by HOW FAR you got, exactly the
intent. NOT too generous (floor is a trivial 3 on failure), NOT too stingy
(progress accrues every room).

Sim corroboration: across 1800 bot A0 lives (which die early, ~room 5, few mobs),
mean renown/life = **25.4** — consistent with a population dominated by shallow
deaths (base ~10-20) with a tail of deeper runs pulling the mean up. A flat
economy would show a much tighter, higher floor; this shows the intended
low-floor / progress-tail shape.

> — the AI floor never clears Ch6, so no life ascends. The multiplier ladder is
> code-confirmed but its realized payout shape needs a competent-bot pass.

Faint directional signals only (n too small to tune on):
- barbarian, 5-7 legendaries: reached Ch5 25% (1 of 4 lives) vs 0% bare.
- barbarian / ranger, 1-2 legendaries: reached Ch5 ~1.5% vs 0% bare.

These lean the right way (more legendaries → marginally deeper) but every
clear% is 0.0% and n≤4 in the meaningful buckets. The standing AI-floor caveat
applies: the bot underplays, so absolute gating is unreadable. To measure
whether gear opens Ch6, this needs a competent-bot policy (cf. caster-AI #211)
or far higher N reaching the back half — NOT a content change.

Relative read that IS valid: class depth order (barb deepest → rogue shallowest)
and legendary-banking rate (barb 2.4/soul ≫ rogue 0.3/soul) track the known
class band and loot-drop exposure. No anomaly.

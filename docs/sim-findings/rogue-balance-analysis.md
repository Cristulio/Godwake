## TL;DR

**The sim does not reproduce the "auto-healing immortal" playtest report.** A bare-soul Rogue dies in 100% of 50-run cells at L1, L3, L5, and L7. A "loaded" Rogue (defensively-optimal shrine pick at all eight shrines) also dies in 100% of cells. HP delta per encounter is consistently negative (−0.6 to −10.1). **No balance lever is applied in this PR.**

## What the numbers say

1. **The Rogue is not immortal in this sim.** 0 / 400 runs survived the Godwake delve. The most successful cohort (L7 loaded) averages 1.0 chapters cleared out of 4.
2. **Hide dominates Cunning Action picks (84–91%).** The Hide → Sneak Attack loop is the engine the Rogue runs on, exactly as the brief predicted.
3. **Sneak Attack fires reliably.** Roughly 1 per encounter at L5+, gated correctly by `sneakAttackUsedThisTurn`. The trigger is not too generous — it's working as designed.
4. **`extraTempHpPerRoom` is the single most-picked blessing pattern.** At L7 loaded, 44% of runs picked Lathander's Dawn (+3 temp HP per room) and 40% picked Ilmater's Crown (+2 temp HP per room). These aggregate (sum into `blessingMods.extraTempHpPerRoom`), so a Rogue who picks both gets the higher of `current.temp` and **5** every room — the single biggest stacking lever in the blessing system.
5. **Despite that, HP delta per encounter is still negative everywhere.** Even with both temp-HP blessings, average damage taken (38–115 per cell) exceeds average heal (rest + potion + temp HP) per encounter. The "immortal" feel is not in the math the sim sees.

## Why the playtest may still have felt immortal

The sim ignores three things that the playtest run almost certainly had:

- **Grove upgrades (`permanentBonuses`).** Iron Will (+5 HP), Mantle of the Wakened (+5 HP), Whetstone (+1 damage), First Cut (+1 first-attack damage), Knife in the Dark (+1d6 Sneak), Bleed-Out (+1 vs wounded). A returning player with these stacked has +10 HP and +3–5 damage per swing on top of the bare class. With Knife in the Dark, Sneak Attack at L7 is 4d6+1d6 = average 17.5 — enough to one-shot most Ch1 enemies before they swing back.
- **Localized window, not global.** Looking at the per-cell breakdown, the L5 loaded Rogue averages 14 rooms cleared and only dies 14× in Ch1 vs 33× in Ch2. **Ch1 with a temp-HP blessing is approximately self-sustaining** — HP delta hovers near zero through the first chapter, then collapses in Ch2 when monster damage scales up. The "feels immortal" feedback may be describing a real but bounded Ch1 window, not a global problem.
- **Events skipped.** The sim treats event rooms as no-ops. In actual play, many events grant HP back, gold for potions, or blessings — all of which the sim under-counts.

## Why no fix is shipped

The four levers in the brief don't fit the data:

| Lever | Brief framing | Reality |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| Hide cooldown | Make Hide cost more / cooldown | Sim already shows Rogue dying. Nerfing Hide pushes the floor further down — wrong direction. |
| Sneak Attack first-hit-only | Tighten the trigger | Already gated by `sneakAttackUsedThisTurn` (once per turn). Sim fires ~1 per encounter. No regression here. |
| Rest rooms 70 → 60% | Gentle nerf | Rogue is already below break-even on HP. Cutting rest healing makes the floor worse, not the ceiling lower. |
| Steel Yourself stacking | Cap with armor reduction | Steel Yourself in the current code grants **save advantage**, not damage reduction (see `src/engine/combat/cunningAction.ts:55`). The brief's framing doesn't match the code. |

The honest read of the data: **the Rogue is, if anything, under-tuned in vacuum.** What the playtest described is most likely a Grove-upgraded soul plus a temp-HP blessing pick in Ch1, which the sim cannot reproduce without extending the harness. Shipping a nerf based on one un-modeled report would push the Rogue's already-dire 100% death rate even further.

## Recommendation

1. **Do not nerf the Rogue's combat loop in this PR.** Hide / Sneak Attack / Cunning Action are doing what they're supposed to.
2. **If `extraTempHpPerRoom` is the lever:** consider a soft change — make the per-room grant `max(current, max-of-individual-blessing)` instead of `max(current, sum)`, so stacking Lathander's Dawn + Ilmater's Crown grants 3 temp HP per room (the higher of 3 and 2), not 5. This affects every class equally and matches 5e RAW for temp HP. **Not applied in this PR** — it touches the blessing engine, not the Rogue, and should be scoped to its own change with a balance pass across all three classes.
3. **Extend the sim to model Grove upgrades** before the next Rogue-balance pass, so the "loaded soul" matches a returning player's loadout. Without that, we can't distinguish "class is broken" from "this particular loadout is broken."
4. **Ask the playtester for the loadout** that felt immortal — race / class / Grove upgrades / blessings / Ch reached. One character-sheet screenshot would resolve more than another 50 sim runs.

## Cunning Action distribution

Hide dominates at 84–91%. Dash is **never picked** (0% across all cells) because the AI never bursts — the Rogue's single-attack action and Hide's per-fight scarcity make Dash strictly worse than Hide for damage. Steel Yourself is also never picked (no monster save-cast on the path the sim took). If you want Dash and Steel to feel useful, the lever is buffing them or surfacing them in the AI's UI prompts — **not** nerfing Hide.
